# Privacy Policy

## Data we collect
We collect the account details you share, the repositories you submit for analysis, and usage logs that show how the product is performing. Payment details are handled by Stripe; we only receive the identifiers needed to reconcile invoices.

## How we use data
We use your information to authenticate requests, run audiobook jobs, and improve reliability. Aggregated analytics help us spot errors and prioritize roadmap work. We never sell personal data.

## Storage and retention
Repository archives, generated audio, and transcripts are stored in AWS S3 with encryption at rest. Database records live in managed PostgreSQL. We keep operational data for as long as your account is active or as required for compliance obligations.

## Sharing
Vendors that help us run the product—such as cloud hosting, payments, and observability—may process your data on our behalf under contractual safeguards. We may disclose data if required by law or to address security incidents.

## Your choices
You can request account deletion or data export by emailing privacy@codebaseaudiobook.com. Deleting your account removes active jobs and stored outputs unless law requires us to retain them longer.

## Updates
We will post updates to this policy in the repository and call out major changes in release notes. Continuing to use the service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
