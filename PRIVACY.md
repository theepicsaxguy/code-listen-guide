# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 28, 2024

We built Codebase Audiobook to help teams learn faster without compromising privacy. This policy outlines the data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can stay in control.

## Information we collect
- **Account details:** name, email address, and password hash when you sign up.
- **Payment data:** billing details handled by Stripe. We see limited card metadata and never store card numbers.
- **Repository content:** source files you upload or reference. We process these files to create outlines, scripts, and audio, then keep them only as long as needed to deliver your project.
- **Usage analytics:** event logs, request metadata, and rate-limit counters that help us secure the platform and debug issues.

## How we use information
- Authenticate you and protect accounts with JWT-based sessions.
- Enforce security measures such as rate limiting and anomaly detection.
- Provide paid features, manage credits, and send invoices or receipts.
- Improve reliability by monitoring performance and troubleshooting errors.

## Sharing
We share data with trusted subprocessors only when necessary:
- Stripe for payments
- Cloud hosting providers for storage and compute
- Email providers for support messages and essential notifications

We do not sell personal information.

## Retention
- Account data remains until you delete your account or request removal.
- Repository assets are deleted within 30 days of job completion unless you ask us to keep them longer for future updates.
- Log data used for security and auditing is retained for up to 12 months.

## Your choices
- Update account details in-app or by contacting support@codebaseaudiobook.com.
- Request data deletion or export. We will confirm the request within 30 days.
- Opt out of non-essential emails through the unsubscribe link provided.

## Security commitments
We apply TLS for data in transit, hardened CORS rules, strict content security policies, and per-client rate limiting to reduce abuse. Access to production systems is limited to trained staff with multifactor authentication.

## Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy as the product evolves. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in-app or by email.
